# Portfolio

Ali Saleh's personal portfolio, which presents his career to recruiters and hiring managers as the story of a hiker climbing a mountain.

## Language

### The story

**Climb**:
The telling of Ali's career as a mountain ascent, which a Visitor follows from the Trailhead up to High Camp.
_Avoid_: Journey, timeline, story page

**Hiker**:
The figure on the mountain who stands for Ali throughout the Climb.
_Avoid_: Character, avatar, climber

**Stage**:
One of the five ordered parts of the Climb, each covering one period of Ali's career and carrying one Challenge. In order: Trailhead, Long Approach, Steep Switch, Ridge, High Camp.
_Avoid_: Section, chapter, step, level

**Challenge**:
The real hardship Ali faced during a Stage, told in his own words.
_Avoid_: Obstacle, struggle

**Trailhead**:
The first Stage: the B.Sc. (2016) and M.Sc. (2018) in Management Information Systems.

**Long Approach**:
The second Stage: the freelance healthcare backend years, 2019 to 2025.

**Steep Switch**:
The third Stage: leaving backend work for AI through the SE Factory bootcamp in 2026.

**Ridge**:
The fourth Stage: the six Projects.

**High Camp**:
The fifth and last Stage: leading two engineers at Kirelo. It is where the Climb ends, with the Summit in view.

**Summit**:
The peak of the mountain, which stays ahead of the Hiker and is never reached within the Climb.
_Avoid_: Peak, top, destination

**Project**:
One of the six AI automation and agent projects from Ali's CV, shown on the Ridge.
_Avoid_: Case study, work item

**Summary**:
The plain page that presents Ali's career without the Climb, for a Visitor in a hurry.
_Avoid_: Résumé page, quick view, about page

**Video CV**:
Ali's recorded video introduction, shown on the Summary and at High Camp.
_Avoid_: Intro video, showreel

**Portrait**:
The photograph of Ali shown on the Summary and beside the contact form.
_Avoid_: Avatar, cover photo, headshot

### The game

**Expedition**:
One Visitor's playthrough of the Climb as a game, in which they make a Decision at each Stage instead of only reading. It is an optional second way up the same mountain.
_Avoid_: Game session, run, playthrough

**Decision**:
The real choice Ali made when facing a Stage's Challenge, which an Expedition asks the Visitor to make before revealing what Ali chose and why.
_Avoid_: Choice, question, quiz

**Cairn**:
A hidden marker on the mountain that reveals one fact about Ali when a Visitor finds it.
_Avoid_: Easter egg, collectible, discovery

**Expedition Log**:
The record a Visitor builds during an Expedition: one stamp per Stage completed, plus the Cairns found.
_Avoid_: Score, achievements, badges

**Guide**:
The mountain guide who offers hints during an Expedition.
_Avoid_: Assistant, chatbot, helper

### Visitors

**Visitor**:
An anonymous person viewing the site. A Visitor is never identified or tracked across days.
_Avoid_: User, guest, lead

**Visit**:
One Visitor's single session on the site, from arrival to leaving.
_Avoid_: Session, hit, page view

**Progress**:
How far up the mountain a Visit got, measured as the highest Stage it reached.
_Avoid_: Scroll depth, completion
